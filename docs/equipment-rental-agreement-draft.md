## Proposed terms

1. **Use and custody.** Renter is responsible for the identified equipment and accessories from handoff until return and inspection. Renter will use it only for its intended purpose, follow the manufacturer's instructions, and will not transfer, subrent, alter, or repair it without our approval.
2. **Condition and safe operation.** We and Renter will document condition and included parts at pickup and return. Renter will stop using equipment that appears damaged or unsafe and contact us promptly. Renter will use appropriate protective gear and keep children and untrained users away.
3. **Item-specific precautions.** A generator must be operated outdoors, away from doors, windows, and vents, according to its manual. The auger, ladder, and scaffold must be assembled and operated within their manufacturer limits and instructions. Renter is responsible for choosing a suitable work area and complying with applicable site rules.
4. **Return and extensions.** Renter will return all equipment and accessories at the agreed time and location. An extension requires our approval before the scheduled return. Additional rental charges, if any, will be agreed before an extension. [Decide a late-return rule and any grace period.]
5. **Damage, loss, and cleaning.** Renter will report damage, loss, theft, or malfunction as soon as possible. Renter is responsible for loss or damage beyond ordinary wear to the extent allowed by law, with charges based on documented repair or replacement costs. [Decide whether to charge a cleaning fee, its amount or calculation, and treatment of fuel/consumables.]
6. **Deposit and payment.** The agreed rental charges and deposit are shown above. [Specify when the deposit is collected, whether it is credited to the rental, when it is returned, and how documented charges are handled. State any saved-card authorization separately and plainly.]
7. **Incidents.** Renter will stop operation, seek emergency help when needed, and notify us as soon as safely possible of an injury, accident, theft, fire, or other incident involving the equipment.
8. **Agreement and signature.** This agreement, the completed rental details, and documented condition report form the rental record. The renter confirms the equipment, dates, price, and terms before signing electronically. Georgia law recognizes electronic signatures; preserve the signed version and an audit record.
